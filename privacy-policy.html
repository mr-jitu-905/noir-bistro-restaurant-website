1. Introduction

Noir Bistro (“we,” “our,” or “us”) is committed to honoring and protecting the privacy of our patrons and online visitors. This Privacy Policy outlines our transparent protocols regarding the collection, handling, and protection of information obtained through our official website and dining reservation platforms.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information provided directly by you as well as technical device logs during site interaction:

  • Reservation & Contact Data: Name, email address, telephone number, dining party size, requested dates/times, and dietary preferences.
  • Event Inquiries: Organization names, celebration types, and bespoke menu requests.
  • Technical Data: Browser types, operating systems, referring URLs, IP addresses, and session interaction metrics.

3. How Information Is Used

All information gathered is utilized strictly to provide and enhance our hospitality offerings:

  • Confirming and managing your dining and private salon bookings.
  • Accommodating medical allergies, dietary requirements, and commemorative requests.
  • Transmitting booking updates and answering inquiries.
  • Optimizing website responsiveness, layout stability, and user experience.

4. Information Sharing & Disclosure

Noir Bistro does not sell, rent, monetize, or trade guest personal information to any third parties for marketing purposes. Data is accessed solely by authorized hospitality staff and essential technical infrastructure providers under strict non-disclosure obligations.

5. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

Our website may utilize essential and analytics cookies to maintain user preferences and evaluate aggregate traffic patterns. You may configure your web browser to decline cookies at any time; however, some interactive features (such as form memory) may function with reduced convenience.

6. Data Security & Storage

We deploy industry-standard SSL encryption and administrative security controls to safeguard your data from unauthorized access, loss, alteration, or disclosure.

7. Third-Party External Links

Our website may provide hyperlinks to third-party services (such as social media networks or map providers). Noir Bistro is not responsible for the privacy practices or content of external third-party destinations.

8. Your Rights & Choices

Depending on your jurisdiction, you maintain the right to inspect, correct, update, or request the deletion of your personal contact records stored within our reservation archives.

9. Children's Privacy

Our digital services are not directed to children under 13 years of age, and we do not knowingly solicit or collect identifiable information from minors.

10. Policy Updates & Contact Information

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically to reflect operational changes. Continued use of the website represents acceptance of the latest terms.
